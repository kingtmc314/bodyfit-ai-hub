# v3.14 Product and running-model research notes

## Calomei interaction principles

CaLoMei centres meal capture around a single, low-friction action: take a food photo, let AI identify the food and estimate its nutrition, then show calories and macros prominently in the daily view. The BodyFit revision should therefore lead with a prominent photo/log action, an immediately legible daily calorie budget, and macro progress rather than making the user navigate to a separate analysis flow. The app will retain BodyFit's existing manual entry, favourite foods, historical date navigation and review/edit capabilities.

Source: [CaLoMei App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/calomei-calorie-tracker/id6759828348)

## Gymwork interaction principles

Gymwork emphasises a guided workout flow: choose or create a routine, follow exercise order, log sets/reps/load with minimal interruption, start an automatic rest timer, compare with history, and inspect volume/RPE/muscle-group progress. The BodyFit revision should make the active session the primary surface, make each exercise a compact editable set table, present the next set clearly, preserve equipment and muscle organisation, and provide prior-performance context. BodyFit will use its existing exercise data and will not copy Gymwork branding, proprietary media, or content.

Source: [Gymwork App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/gymwork-workout-tracker-log/id1563624436)

## Running energy-system simulator model

The simulator must be labelled as an educational estimate, not a clinical or laboratory measurement. All three systems work concurrently; their relative contribution changes with effort duration and intensity. A useful practical model uses an early ATP-PCr peak, a glycolytic rise that is most material in approximately 10-second to a few-minute work, and an aerobic ramp that dominates longer sustained running. Physiological estimates should be calibrated by a runner's historic pace, average/max HR, cadence, recent volume, and available body mass/HRV/sleep signals rather than presented as measured VO2 or lactate values.

For the selectable distances, a simple event profile can interpolate plausible system curves from 100 m / 200 m (ATP-PCr-heavy), 400–800 m (higher glycolytic demand and lactate), 1500–3000 m (increasing aerobic contribution), and steady 10 km / half-marathon / marathon (aerobic-dominant). The UI should show uncertainty and explain that predicted finish time is an estimate from logged data.

Sources: [Canada.ca energy systems overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/maple-leaf/rcaf/2020/10/track-and-field-for-masters-athletes-6-understanding-energy-systems.html), [Weyand et al. oxygen deficit study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7808253/)

## Weather adjustment

Weather should be an explicit optional simulation input. Research on elite and well-trained endurance events identifies air temperature as the strongest of the considered weather predictors, followed by relative humidity, solar radiation and wind speed; therefore the UI will derive a conservative environmental load multiplier from temperature, humidity and wind. It should never claim a medical heat-stress assessment. Any time/pace result will show a weather-adjusted estimate alongside the baseline.

Source: [Mantzios et al., Effects of Weather Parameters on Endurance Running Performance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8677617/)
